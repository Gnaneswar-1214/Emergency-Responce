Final Recommendation

🏆 Priority Queue (Heap)

Based on our metrics, the Heap is the optimal data structure for the Emergency Response Dispatch System. It maintains excellent O(log N) insertion and extraction time constants even as dataset size scales up to 50,000+ entries. Although Hash Map provides O(1) inserts, its Top-K and Max-Extraction fall apart at scale, hitting O(N log N) or O(N). The BST performs well but has higher constant factors due to expensive object rotations. Sorted array is inefficient for O(N) inserts.
